Install on Android

Aurix comes straight from us, not the Play Store, so Android likes to say hello with a quick check first. Here's how the next minute goes.

  1. DownloadTap Download APK. Chrome gives a quick heads-up before saving any app file, it's just its routine. Tap Download anyway and it's on its way.
  2. Open the fileWhen it's done, tap Open. You'll also find AurixV7.apk in your Downloads.
  3. One small permissionAndroid asks once if Chrome can install apps. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, step back and tap Install.
  4. Play Protect says hiIt may take a quick look because Aurix isn't from the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway. The full code is open on GitHub if you're curious.
  5. Press playOpen Aurix, search a song and enjoy.
  6. If a song won't playNow and then you may see "track couldn't be resolved". Just skip to the next song and carry on.
